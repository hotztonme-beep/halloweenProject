HALLOWEEN

St. Peter Catholic Church has roots in North Ridgeville dating back to the 1800s. According to the reported legend, the strangest thing happens when the sanctuary is completely quiet. People have said they've heard footsteps walking along the aisle. Not footsteps outside. Not somebody coming through a side door. Footsteps inside the church. The sound reportedly moves along the aisle as though someone is walking through the empty sanctuary. And then it stops. The unsettling part is that the story doesn't describe an apparition appearing afterward. There's simply the sound of someone—or something—walking through a supposedly empty church. That detail has been repeated in multiple accounts of Lorain County hauntings. O Ohio Exploration Society +1 There's no reliable evidence establishing that the footsteps are supernatural, so this is best treated as a local ghost legend/report, not a verified paranormal event.
